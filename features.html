Everything Voyager does

No paywall — tracking, timeline, map, all nine insight lenses, mileage, trips, fitness, proof and export are all free, all on your device. Here's each one: what it does, why it matters, and how it works.

Capture your day

An automatic, battery-aware record of where you go — and honest about the seams.

📍Location tracking

What

A background service that quietly logs your movements with a persistent notification while it's active.

Why

A useful timeline can't ask you to press "record" every time you leave — but always-on GPS would drain the battery.

How

Adaptive sampling changes how often it reads GPS based on whether you're still, walking, or driving, with a near-zero-power dormant tier woken by a hardware motion sensor. Start, pause, and stop are always in your control.

🧭Timeline

What

A chronological day of visits and journeys — drive, transit, cycle, walk — with real place names.

Why

Your history should read like a story you can trust, not a smooth line that quietly papers over missing data.

How

When tracking is interrupted you see an honest gap with its reason. Tap "Why?" on any row to see the evidence behind the classification, or reclassify a segment yourself.

🗺️Map

What

Your day's routes and visit markers on a clean map, synced two-way with the timeline.

Why

Seeing the shape of a day on a map is often the fastest way to remember it.

How

Built on MapLibre + OpenStreetMap — no Google Maps API. Tap a timeline row to fly the map to it, or tap the map to scroll the timeline.

🏷️Places & geocoding

What

Turns raw coordinates into names you recognise — Home, Work, that café — never a bare "Unknown place".

Why

Coordinates aren't memories; names are. But a confidently wrong address is worse than an honest approximation.

How

An on-device-first naming chain falls back to "Near [neighbourhood]" rather than a wrong street, and only shows the precision the evidence supports. Places you visit often resist fading; a review queue lets you confirm or rename the rest in a tap.

Understand it

Your history, read back to you — honestly.

📈Insights — nine lenses

What

Overview, Weekly, Highlights, Patterns, Rhythm/Sleep, Movement, Balance, Carbon, and Anomalies — plus commute analysis and a next-place prediction.

Why

The value of a timeline isn't the raw log; it's what it tells you about how your life actually happens.

How

Each lens is computed on-device from your own history, told as a short "storybook" chapter. Predictions are only ever about routines you've actually built — it anticipates without pretending to know the future.

🏃Activities & fitness

What

A private fitness tracker — record runs, walks, rides and hikes with a live map, splits, elevation, and personal records.

Why

Fitness apps are some of the most privacy-invasive things on a phone; your training shouldn't live on someone's server.

How

Auto-pause keeps your pace honest at traffic lights; elevation is smoothed to reject jitter; and you can save a favourite stretch as a "race-yourself" segment — efforts matched on the device, no cloud leaderboard.

Prove it

Audit-ready records you can actually defend.

🚗Mileage & vehicles

What

Turn detected drives into a tax-ready mileage log with IRS/HMRC-style deductions in your currency, plus fuel-cost and CO₂ per vehicle.

Why

A mileage claim has to stand up to scrutiny — and dedicated mileage apps charge a subscription for this one feature.

How

Classify a drive with a tap; the deduction math is exact, and every row carries the underlying GPS evidence — not just a PDF total. Export to CSV.

🧳Trips

What

Auto-detected multi-day journeys away from home, each openable as a shareable story.

Why

Trips are the memories worth keeping — and they shouldn't require a separate, cloud-locked travel app.

How

Detected from your timeline and broken correctly across data blackouts, so a gap in tracking doesn't split one trip into two.

🛡️Proof / evidence hub

What

One place framing your records as audit-ready — mileage, trips, and export, each backed by evidence.

Why

"Trust me" isn't good enough when the record needs to convince an accountant, an auditor, or your future self.

How

Every drive, trip, and export carries its GPS evidence and the rule version that produced it — all computed on-device, and it never leaves your phone.

📷Day Story

What

Your own device photos for a day, pinned to the places you visited — a scrollable memory.

Why

A place and a photo together are the memory; but your photos should never be uploaded to make that happen.

How

Reads photo timestamps and locations on-device and matches them to your visits. Photos are never copied or sent anywhere.

Own it & protect it

Your data, portable and private by construction.

🔎Search & import/export

What

Full-text search across places and days with filters, plus export to GPX, GeoJSON, CSV, and JSON — and import from Google Timeline.

Why

Owning your data means being able to find it, take it anywhere, or delete it entirely — and bringing your old history in when a cloud service abandons you.

How

A maintained on-device search index powers the queries; the Google Timeline importer is the migration wedge — "bring your Timeline home."

🩺Reliability

What

A dedicated screen that explains aggressive OEM battery-killing and offers a self-test for tracking gaps.

Why

On many Android phones the biggest threat to a timeline isn't code — it's the manufacturer's battery optimiser silently killing background apps.

How

It detects your device, links to the exact exemption steps, and a background health-check restarts tracking if it's ever silently killed.

🔒Privacy & security

What

On-device encryption, a biometric app lock, and a mode that blocks screenshots and hides the app in Recents.

Why

A location history is one of the most sensitive things you own; privacy has to be the architecture, not a setting you might forget.

How

The whole database is encrypted at all times, keyed to your device's hardware. No accounts, no analytics, no ads — the network is used only for optional place-name lookups and map tiles.

🧩One record, many jobs

What

Memory, tax-grade proof, and habit insight — all from a single on-device timeline.

Why

Today these are separate apps that each re-track you and silo your data.

How

Because everything derives from the same evidence base, adding a "job" costs no extra tracking and no new data silo.
