Every feature free

Everything Voyager does

Voyager ships with no paywall. Tracking, timeline, map, all nine insight lenses, mileage, trips, fitness, proof and export — all free, all on your device.

📍Location tracking

A battery-aware foreground service with adaptive sampling and a near-zero passive tier. Fuses GPS, activity recognition, an accelerometer signature, barometer, step counter and Wi-Fi. Real start / pause / stop.

🧭Timeline

Visits and movement segments (drive, transit, cycle, walk), colour-coded and timezone-aware, with honest GAP rows. Tap “Why?” to see the evidence, or reclassify a segment yourself.

🗺️Map

A dark cartographic basemap that renders your day's routes and visit markers, with two-way focus sync to the timeline. Powered by MapLibre + OpenStreetMap — no Google Maps API.

🏷️Places & geocoding

Turns coordinates into names you recognise — your own name, a category, a provider name, or “Near [neighbourhood]”. Recurring places resist confidence decay; a review queue lets you confirm the rest.

📈Insights — 9 lenses

Overview, Weekly, Highlights, Patterns, Rhythm/Sleep, Movement, Balance, Carbon and Anomalies — plus commute analysis, next-place prediction, a heatmap and a Year-in-Review.

🏃Activities & fitness

A private, on-device fitness tracker: record runs, walks, rides and hikes with a live map, auto-pause, per-kilometre splits, elevation, personal records and race-yourself segments.

🚗Mileage & vehicles

Classify drives (business / personal), get IRS/HMRC-style deductions in your currency, and export a log where every row carries its GPS evidence. Vehicles add fuel-cost and CO₂ estimates.

🧳Trips

Auto-detected multi-day journeys away from home, broken correctly across data blackouts, each openable as a shareable story.

🛡️Proof / evidence hub

Audit-ready by design — every drive, trip and export carries its GPS evidence and rule version, computed on-device and never leaving your phone.

📷Day Story

Your own device photos for a day, pinned to the places you visited — a scrollable memory, entirely local. Photos are never uploaded.

🔎Search & export

Full-text search across places and days with date/category/mode filters. Export to Voyager JSON, GPX, GeoJSON and CSV — and import from Google Timeline.

🔒Privacy & security

SQLCipher full-database encryption, a biometric app lock, a screenshot-blocking secure mode, no accounts, no analytics, no ads. Network is used only for optional geocoding and map tiles.

Under the hood

A transparent heuristic engine — explainable, not a black box.

Raw samples flow through a single serial pipeline — normalise → Kalman-filter → quality-score → dedup → commit-state → segment — with a gap watchdog and crash-recovery state store, so the timeline stays correct and every row can explain itself. Around twenty WorkManager jobs handle place discovery, geocoding, trip detection, rollups, recaps, confidence decay, retention, integrity checks and search indexing.

Built with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose + Material 3, Hilt, Room (encrypted) and MapLibre — clean architecture, one engineer, and a deliberate design.
