The architecture, at a glance

Clean layers, and an 8-stage single-writer pipeline that's lock-free by construction.

The pipeline, stage by stage

Every GPS sample flows through these on one coroutine draining one channel — so the stages hold state with no locks, and whole classes of race conditions simply can't happen.

Persist raw
The raw fix is written append-only before any filtering — evidence is never lost.
Dedup (raw)
An accuracy-aware noise floor kills stationary jitter that was producing 48+ samples/min — before it can smear the filter.
Anti-spoof
A jump implying > Mach 1 is a teleport; dropped before the Kalman filter so a spoofed point can't corrupt its reference.
Kalman
A 4-state constant-velocity filter with a Joseph-form covariance update — stable across 10,000+ iterations on multi-day tracks.
Quality
Discards mock/low-accuracy fixes, with Doze-aware staleness so Samsung/Xiaomi batching isn't mistaken for GPS loss.
Fuse activity
Merges activity-recognition, Kalman speed, step cadence, and an accelerometer-variance signal — every label a fusion, never a single source.
Visit & segment
Hysteresis-based arrival/departure (honest dwell times), then movement segmentation — with a live in-memory snapshot for the UI.
Honest gaps
A 60-second watchdog writes reason-coded GAP rows (DORMANT vs GPS_LOSS vs PROCESS_DEAD) — missing data is a first-class row, never a faked line.

The moat: evidence, not a black box

Voyager stores why alongside what.

🧾Counter-evidence

Every inferred label carries its supporting metrics and why competing labels were rejected. Tap a “Walking” segment and see its speed range, sample count, confidence — and why “Cycling” was ruled out. No competitor exposes this.

🔒Encrypted, device-bound

SQLCipher is always on; the key is derived from a hardware-backed Android Keystore key that never leaves the device. Privacy is the architecture, not a setting.

🧩One record, many jobs

The same on-device timeline powers memory, tax-grade proof, and habit insight — no re-tracking, no per-feature silos.

🕳️Honest by default

Honest gaps, correctable inferences, and evidence on tap. The design principle: “trust is built at the seams.”

Honest by design

What it isn't (yet)

Voyager is a heuristic engine, not ML — deliberately, because every inference must be explainable and correctable. It's pre-production, so its battery and accuracy figures are rigorous estimates, not field data. Four of seven planned differentiators are shipped; the rest are the roadmap. Knowing exactly where the edges are is the point.
