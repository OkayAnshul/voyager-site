Why Voyager is different

Four things that don't come standard anywhere else.

1
Local-first & encrypted by default. Your history lives in an SQLCipher-encrypted database whose key never leaves your phone's hardware keystore. Privacy isn't a setting — it's the architecture.
2
Evidence-backed. Every visit, drive and place can explain exactly why it was inferred — including counter-evidence — so a record can be defended to yourself, an accountant, or an auditor.
3
Honest gaps. When tracking is interrupted, Voyager shows a clear gap instead of faking a smooth line.
4
One record, many jobs. The same on-device history powers your timeline, mileage log, trip book, fitness records and insights.
