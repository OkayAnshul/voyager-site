How it works

Four steps, all on your phone — no sign-up, no server.

Capture

A battery-aware background service quietly logs where you go, adapting how often it reads GPS to what you're doing.

Understand

A real-time pipeline turns the noisy stream into a truthful timeline — named places, transport modes, and honest gaps.

Prove

Every visit, drive, and trip carries its evidence, so you can export an audit-ready mileage log or trip in a tap.

Own

It's encrypted on your device. Export it, import your old Google Timeline, or delete everything — always your call.

Why Voyager is different

Four things that don't come standard anywhere else.

1
Local-first & encrypted by default. Your history lives in an encrypted database whose key never leaves your phone's hardware keystore. There's no cloud to breach and no account to leak. Privacy isn't a setting — it's the architecture.
2
Evidence-backed. Every visit, drive and place can explain exactly why it was inferred — including counter-evidence, the reasons the alternatives were rejected. That turns a black box into something you, an accountant, or a court can actually trust.
3
Honest gaps. When tracking is interrupted — battery, a manufacturer's optimiser, lost signal — Voyager shows a clear, reason-coded gap instead of inventing a smooth line. Trust is built at the seams.
4
One record, many jobs. The same on-device history powers your timeline, your mileage log, your trip book, your fitness records and your insights — with no re-tracking and no per-feature silo.

Questions

Is my data really private?

Yes. Your history is stored encrypted on your device and never sent to us or anyone else. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no ads. The network is used only for optional place-name lookups and map tiles — never to upload your timeline.

Does it need the cloud or an account?

No. Voyager works fully offline (aside from optional geocoding), with no sign-up. To move to a new phone, you export an encrypted file and import it — your data never passes through a server.

Is it actually free?

Every feature is free — tracking, timeline, map, all nine insight lenses, mileage, trips, fitness, proof, and export. No paywall.

What do I need to run it?

An Android 8.0+ phone. Location and activity permissions power the timeline; a persistent notification shows whenever tracking is active, and you can start, pause, or stop anytime.

Can I bring my Google Timeline history?

Yes — import your Google Timeline JSON and Voyager brings it onto your device.
